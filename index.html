Priority tier

Every opportunity is scored for student-startup fit (student/university focus, non-dilutive money, stage match, support offered) and placed in a tier. Tiers are a starting point, not a verdict — a Tier 3 program in your exact sector can beat a generic Tier 1.

Tier 1 Excellent fit
Built for student or university founders. Non-dilutive grants, prizes or accelerator funding with a clear stage match. Apply to these first.
Tier 2 Strong fit
Students are clearly eligible and competitive, but the program is broader (regional, national or industry-wide) or the award is smaller.
Tier 3 Moderate fit
Open to students but not focused on them. Often needs traction, a research base or a specific industry. Worth checking if it matches your sector.
Tier 4 Secondary
Loosely related, equity-based, or aimed at later-stage or non-student teams. Included for completeness.

How this list is built

Each opportunity is reviewed by the UC San Diego entrepreneurship team and tagged for student startup fit, founder stage, eligibility and priority tier. All active opportunities are in the database; the page opens on Tier 1–2 and the 20 nearest deadlines, and you choose which tiers and how many results to show.

Save a view to come back to the same search later, copy its link to share it, or subscribe to alerts so we email you when matching opportunities open or are about to close.

  • Open Applications are accepted now, or on a rolling basis.
  • Closes in 14 days A confirmed deadline is approaching.
  • Expected The date is projected from last year's cycle and has not been confirmed yet.
  • Closed The most recent cycle has passed; check back for the next round.
