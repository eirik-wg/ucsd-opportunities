How this list is built

Each opportunity is reviewed by the UC San Diego entrepreneurship team and tagged for student startup fit, founder stage, eligibility and priority tier. Only Tier 1 and Tier 2 opportunities that are currently active appear on this page.

  • Open Applications are accepted now, or on a rolling basis.
  • Closes in 14 days A confirmed deadline is approaching.
  • Expected The date is projected from last year's cycle and has not been confirmed yet.
  • Closed The most recent cycle has passed; check back for the next round.
